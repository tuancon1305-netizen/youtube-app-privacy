Welcome to TC Automation. Our application automates YouTube channel management, including video uploading and metadata editing.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how TC Automation handles your data when integrating with Google Services.

1. Information Collection & Use

Our application requests access to the following YouTube API scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl

These scopes are used solely to automate video uploading, edit video metadata, and manage video content for the channel owner. We do not store any personal user data on external servers.

2. Data Sharing & Protection

All authentication tokens are stored locally on the owner's execution machine. No user data is shared with third parties.
