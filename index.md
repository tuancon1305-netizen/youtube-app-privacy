    <h2>Privacy Policy for TC Automation</h2>
    <p>This Privacy Policy describes how <strong>TC Automation</strong> handles your data when integrating with Google Services.</p>

    <h3>1. Information Collection & Use</h3>
    <p>Our application requests access to the following YouTube API scopes:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><code>https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload</code></li>
        <li><code>https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl</code></li>
    </ul>
    <p>These scopes are used solely to automate video uploading, edit video metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails), update video details, and manage video content for the channel owner. We do not collect, process, or store any personal user data on external servers.</p>

    <h3>2. Data Sharing & Protection</h3>
    <p>All authentication tokens (OAuth Access and Refresh Tokens) are stored locally on the owner's execution machine. No user data, video content, or channel credentials are ever shared with, sold to, or used by third parties.</p>
