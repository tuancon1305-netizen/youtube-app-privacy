# Privacy Policy for [TC Automation]

This Privacy Policy describes how **[TC Automation]** handles your data when integrating with Google Services.

## 1. Information Collection & Use
Our application requests access to the `youtube.upload` and `youtube.force-ssl` scopes solely to automate video uploading, metadata assignment, and channel management for the channel owner. We do not collect, process, or store any personal user data on external servers.

## 2. Data Sharing & Protection
All authentication tokens (OAuth Access/Refresh Tokens) are stored locally on the owner's execution machine. No user data, video content, or channel credentials are ever shared with, sold to, or used by third parties.
